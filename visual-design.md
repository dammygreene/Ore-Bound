# VISUAL DESIGN

## Direction

Stylized industrial 2.5D.

Avoid generic voxel and photorealistic styles.

## Camera

Orthographic camera with 2.5D depth created through geometry, lighting, shadows and parallax.

## Surface

Industrial garage:
- machinery
- rails
- storage
- refinery
- signs
- warm lighting

## Underground

Layered geology:
- soil
- dirt
- stone
- hard rock
- deep rock
- special geological pockets

Depth changes atmosphere, not guaranteed value.

## Lighting

Use a small number of meaningful lights. Excavator provides local illumination.

## Effects

Dust, debris, sparks, subtle fog and restrained mineral glow.

## Excavator

Strong silhouette, animated drill, tracks, cabin, headlights and mechanical joints.

## Rare discoveries

Diamonds use a strong silhouette, restrained glow, sparkle and unique reveal.
Artifacts should feel mysterious.
