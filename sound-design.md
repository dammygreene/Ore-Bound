# SOUND DESIGN

## Ambient

Surface: machinery, wind, distant activity.

Underground: excavator hum, geological ambience, distant rumbles and cave resonance.

## Excavator

Idle, movement, drill startup, active drill, strain and damaged states.

## Mining

Dirt, rock, hard rock and ore each need distinct impact profiles.

## Scanner

Directional audio with increasing pulse frequency as a target gets closer.

Hazard signals need a separate identity.

## UI

Short open, close, confirm, error, upgrade and warning sounds.

## Rare discovery

Give major discoveries a distinct sound and temporarily reduce ordinary audio intensity.

Important gameplay information must never depend only on sound.
