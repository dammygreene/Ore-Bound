# WORLD DESIGN

The mine is a place, not a reward table.

Every area should contain ordinary material, routes, dead ends, caves, hazards and resource opportunities.

## Generation

Deterministic:
seed + chunk coordinates + generation version.

## Tunnels

Mining removes terrain and creates traversable space.

## Caves

Large spaces can contain resources, artifacts, props and hazards.

## Geological variety

Examples:
- clay
- limestone
- granite
- crystal formation
- abandoned tunnel
- underground water pocket
- fossil layer

## Special structures

Rare:
- abandoned shaft
- forgotten machine
- sealed vault
- ancient chamber
- meteor pocket

## Visibility

Show explored tunnels and nearby terrain. Keep unexplored territory concealed.
