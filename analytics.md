# ANALYTICS

Track aggregate gameplay and performance without unnecessary personal data.

Events:
session_start
mine_enter
tile_mined
scanner_used
resource_discovered
diamond_discovered
hazard_triggered
surface_return
resource_sold
upgrade_purchased
excavator_changed
season_progress
leaderboard_submission

Performance:
FPS bucket, load time, crash/error rate, device class and quality setting.

Do not design mechanics purely to maximize telemetry.
