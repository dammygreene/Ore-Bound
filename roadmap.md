# ROADMAP

## M0 Foundation
Three.js renderer, responsive canvas, camera, input and debug tools.

## M1 Mining Prototype
Excavator, terrain, mining, one resource, surface and return.

## M2 Exploration
Deterministic world, chunks, persistent tunnels, scanner, caves, gold and diamonds.

## M3 Management
Fuel, durability, storage, hazards and repairs.

## M4 Progression
Excavator classes, upgrades and stats.

## M5 Economy
Refinery, selling, storage and marketplace architecture.

## M6 Social
Profiles, leaderboards, discovery feed and share cards.

## M7 Seasons
Seasonal points, challenges and cosmetics.

## M8 Blockchain
Wallet, token utility, ownership and claims.

## M9 Polish
Art, sound, animation, mobile/PC optimization, accessibility, security and launch preparation.
